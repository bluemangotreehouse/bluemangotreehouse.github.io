Our Applications

MoneyMate – Personal Finance Manager

MoneyMate is a personal finance management application designed to help users manage their daily finances.

With MoneyMate, users can:

  • Track daily income and expenses.
  • Organize transactions and monitor spending.
  • Understand their financial activities.
  • Manage their personal financial records.
  • Sign in using their Google account.
  • Back up and restore their financial data using Google Drive, where this feature is enabled.

Our goal: Make personal finance management simple, organized and accessible.

Google Account Integration

MoneyMate uses Google Sign-In to allow users to authenticate using their Google account.

If users choose to enable Google Drive backup, the application uses the requested Google Drive permissions to store and restore their MoneyMate backup data.

Google account access is used only to provide the features users choose to use.
